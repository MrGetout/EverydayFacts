EverydayFacts Uploader

A private command-line tool used only by the owner of the Thai science-explainer channel "จำไว้ว่า... | Everyday Facts" (YouTube @EverydayFacts_E_F and its Facebook Page).

What it does

  • Uploads the owner's own finished videos to the owner's own YouTube channel and Facebook Page.
  • Sets title, description, tags and thumbnail, and schedules the publish time.
  • Checks that it is authorized on the correct channel before uploading.

It runs only on the owner's personal computer. It has no public sign-up, no other users, no server and no database. It does not read or store any data about viewers or other users.
