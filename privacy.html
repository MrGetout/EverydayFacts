Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

EverydayFacts Uploader ("the app") is a private, non-commercial tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel and Facebook Page "จำไว้ว่า... | Everyday Facts" to upload and schedule the owner's own videos. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

Use of YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using the app, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Information handled through YouTube API Services is also subject to the Google Privacy Policy.

What data the app accesses

  • YouTube: the identity of the authorized channel (to confirm it is the correct channel), and permission to upload videos, set their title, description, tags, thumbnail, privacy status and scheduled publish time.
  • Facebook: the list of Pages the owner manages, and permission to publish videos and posts to the owner's own Page.
  • The app does not access, collect or store any data about viewers, subscribers, commenters, followers or any other users.

How data is used and stored

  • Data is used only to publish the owner's own content to the owner's own channel and Page.
  • OAuth tokens are stored only on the owner's personal computer. The app has no server and no database.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising, analytics or profiling.
  • The app does not use cookies or similar tracking technologies.

Revoking access and deleting data

  • Google / YouTube access can be revoked at any time at Google security settings — third-party access.
  • Facebook access can be removed at Facebook Settings → Business Integrations.
  • When access is revoked, the stored tokens stop working and are deleted from the owner's computer. The app keeps no other copy of any data.

Changes

Any change to this policy will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
